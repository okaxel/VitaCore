## Getting Started

VitaCore is a comprehensive health data platform that unifies DNA data, medical records, and real-time wearable metrics into the world's largest global health dataset. To bypass complex international healthcare regulations, VitaCore leverages a decentralized architecture to enforce absolute data sovereignty. Because health data is inherently personal property, individual data sharing cannot be restricted by traditional legal silos—it belongs entirely to the user.

To drive immediate market adoption, VitaCore's initial launch targets international patients and longevity enthusiasts. These high-potential early adopters actively seek out and fund medical experiments; VitaCore enables them to fuel these trials with massive, standardized streams of real-world data. Designed for enterprise scalability, VitaCore features robust data pipelines built for seamless integration by industry giants. Ultimately, VitaCore bridges commercial viability and profound social impact, democratizing advanced medical insights while lowering the cost of healthcare devices and treatments globally.
